Local Media ships with two separate programs that it runs but does not contain:

ffprobe.exe and ffmpeg.exe
  What they are: the file inspector and the converter from FFmpeg, in the build
               published by the Jellyfin project (jellyfin-ffmpeg 8.1.3-2, Windows
               64-bit, GPL build).
  Used for:    ffprobe reads what is inside each video file (length, picture and
               sound formats). ffmpeg repackages files, and converts their sound,
               for devices that cannot play them as they are, and takes subtitles
               out of video files.

               LICENSE.txt in this folder.
  Source code: https://github.com/jellyfin/jellyfin-ffmpeg/tree/v8.1.3-2
  Downloaded from:
               https://github.com/jellyfin/jellyfin-ffmpeg/releases/tag/v8.1.3-2
               and checked against a fixed checksum when this package was built.
