# Your checklist for M1 so far (about 20 minutes)

M1 turns the list of files into a library. It is not finished, but what exists is worth trying on your real files, because your library is the real test of how well names are recognised.

Still true: **many files will not play yet.** The difference is that the app now marks them in advance and says why.

## 1. Get the new build

1. Open the link I give you in the chat and download **LocalMedia-Windows**.
2. Close the old program (close its black window).
3. Unzip the download. The folder holds `localmedia.exe` and, new this time, `ffprobe.exe`. Keep them in the same folder: `ffprobe.exe` is the file inspector the app uses to see what is inside each video.
4. Double-click `localmedia.exe`. Your sign-in, folder and watch history from last time are still there. Windows may show the same warnings as before.

## 2. Look at the library

1. Films and shows should now be separate, with episodes grouped into seasons.
2. Give it a minute: a line near the top counts down while it inspects your files.
3. Files this browser cannot play are marked "Needs converting". Open one: it should say why in plain words.
4. Try the search box with part of a title.

## 3. Add your TMDB key (posters and descriptions)

1. Click **Settings**.
2. Paste the **short** key (32 letters and digits) and click **Save key**.
3. Posters, descriptions and episode names should appear within a minute or two.
4. If a film gets the wrong poster or none, note its file name for me.

## 4. Try a show

1. Watch an episode to the end (or skip to the last few seconds).
2. Go back to the first screen: the next episode should be under "Next up".

## 5. Try a rename

1. In Windows, rename one film file you have already watched, or move it to another folder inside your library.
2. In the app, click **Look for new files**.
3. It should say one file was moved or renamed, and the film should still show as watched.

## 6. On the iPhone

1. Open the app in Safari as before.
2. Check whether the marks differ from the PC. They should: Safari cannot open MKV files at all, but it can play Dolby sound, which Chrome on the PC cannot.

## 7. Send me the results

1. Click **Export support bundle** on the PC and attach the file to our chat. New in it: the titles that were not found online, as read from your file names. That list is how I improve the recognition. It does not hold your key, and it still lists no file that worked.
2. Tell me: anything recognised wrongly (a film shown as an episode, two shows merged, a wrong poster), and whether the rename kept its "watched" mark.
